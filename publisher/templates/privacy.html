Privacy

FORGE Map is a free public map. You don’t need an account to use the map. Optional member chat (Social) uses a Google sign-in and runs on Google Firebase, as described below. FORGE Map is run by K2026598376 (South Africa) (Pty) Ltd, trading as Zone2Freelancers, registration number 2026/598376/07. For questions about your information, email rileynotbishop@gmail.com.

Our Information Officer is Matamela Ramovha (Information Regulator registration number 2026-063320).

Last updated 24 October 2026

The short version

  • FORGE Map’s own code sets no cookies and runs no analytics, advertising or tracking scripts.
  • What you save on the site, such as your Watchlist and settings, stays in your own browser. It is never sent to us.
  • If you join Social, your Google sign-in goes through Firebase, and your display name, messages and any reports you make are stored there.
  • To show the site, your browser also fetches files from a few outside services: our host, fonts, map tiles and code libraries. Like any web server, they receive your IP address when it does. They are listed below.
  • A street address you type into Area check goes to Geoapify, not to us.

What stays in your browser

The site saves these in your browser’s storage, on your device only. They don’t move between your devices, and clearing your browser’s site data removes them.

  • Settings: theme, time zone and how times are shown.
  • Watchlist: the pins and entities you starred.
  • Opened sources: the source links you have opened, so the site can mark them as opened. It keeps the last 500.
  • Area check place: the place, radius and dates you chose, with the point rounded to about 10 metres. It is kept only for your current browser session, unless you tick the box to remember it on this device. The Forget button clears it.
  • Notes on reports: any notes you write on a report page.
  • Social sign-in: if you sign in to Social, Firebase keeps you signed in on this browser until you sign out.

Services your browser connects to

When you open a page, your browser asks these services for files. Each one receives your IP address and standard browser details, such as the page that asked for the file.

  • Vercel hosts the site and serves every page. Vercel privacy policy
  • Google Fonts supplies the site’s fonts, on every page. Google Fonts privacy FAQ
  • jsDelivr supplies open-source code the site runs on, such as the map and search libraries. jsDelivr privacy policy
  • OpenStreetMap supplies the map tiles on the map page. OpenStreetMap Foundation privacy policy
  • Google Firebase code is loaded from gstatic.com on the map page, where it runs Social. Until you sign in, it sends us nothing about you. Privacy and security in Firebase

Address search in Area check

Searching for a place name runs on your device, and nothing is sent. If you type a street address instead, the address and your IP address go to Geoapify, which looks up the spot. FORGE Map never receives what you typed. Geoapify’s privacy policy says it keeps the details of each request, including the IP address, for access control and usage counting. Geoapify privacy policy

Member sign-in and chat (Social)

Social is a members-only chat. Membership is by invitation: anyone can sign in with Google, but you can only chat once we have added you as a member. It runs on Google Firebase.

  • Signing in: you sign in with your Google account through Firebase Authentication, which keeps an account record for you, including your email address. Firebase says it keeps the IP addresses it logs for a few weeks, and other sign-in information until we delete your account. Firebase Authentication processes data in the United States.
  • What we store: whether your membership is active or suspended; the display name you choose (up to 40 characters); your messages (up to 500 characters each) with the time they were sent; and the time and ID of your last message, which we use to make you wait 10 seconds between messages. These are stored in Firebase’s Cloud Firestore database.
  • Who sees what: other members see your messages, when they were sent, and “Member” followed by the last four characters of your account ID. Signed-in members can also read the display name you choose. Your Google email address is not shown in the chat.
  • Reports: you can report another member’s message. A report records your account ID, which message, the reason and the time. Only the site’s owner, as moderator, can read reports. The moderator can delete any message.
  • How long we keep it: there is no set period, and nothing is deleted automatically. You can delete your own messages at any time, and you can change your display name. Everything else stays until we delete it.
  • Deleting your account: the site has no button for this. Email rileynotbishop@gmail.com from the Google account you use for Social, and we will delete your sign-in account, membership, display name, activity record, remaining messages and the reports you made.

Links to other sites

Pins link to the original news reports. A source link opens only after a notice that you’re leaving FORGE Map. The site also links to Ko-fi and to one sponsored site. Once you leave FORGE Map, that site’s own privacy policy applies.

People named in pins

Pins are built from news reports and police statements that are already public, and some of them name people. A pin records what was reported, attributed to whoever said it. It is not a finding about anyone. If you are named or can be identified in a pin, you can ask us to check it, correct it or review it. Email rileynotbishop@gmail.com with the pin’s link. How It Works explains how corrections are made and recorded.

If you email us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write, and we use them only to answer you and deal with your request. We delete correspondence 90 days after the request is resolved, unless an unresolved dispute or legal obligation requires us to keep it longer.

Your rights

You can ask what personal information we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it, by emailing rileynotbishop@gmail.com. If you’re not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa.

Changes

If what the site collects changes, we will update this page and the date at the top first.
