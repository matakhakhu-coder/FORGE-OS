How an entry gets here

  1. Written about a place. A named person writes a note about somewhere they have been, tied to a place on the map.
  2. Checked before it goes up. The note is read and checked before anything is published.
  3. Published on purpose. An entry appears only when it is deliberately published. Nothing is posted automatically.

Journal entries and map pins

A journal entry is a person’s own account. It does not add, change or confirm a pin on the map. Pins keep their own checks, described in How It Works.
